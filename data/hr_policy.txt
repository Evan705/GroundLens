ABC company HR Policy 

Annual leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year

Sick leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of Sick leave per year

Parental leave:
Employees are entitled to 12 weeks of Parental leave per year

Work From Home:
Employee can work from home up to 3 days per week.

Travel Policy:
Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel.
